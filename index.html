About

MD & Researcher

I am a Medical Doctor and postdoctoral researcher with a focus on the intersection of clinical medicine and computational biology. My work bridges molecular and cellular approaches with large-scale data analysis to understand complex diseases.

My primary research focuses on retinal neurodegeneration, extracellular vesicle-based therapeutics, and multi-omics approaches to hematologic malignancies. I combine wet-lab expertise with bioinformatics pipelines to generate clinically translatable insights.

I am passionate about open science and reproducible research, and I use R and Python for data analysis, visualization, and genomic workflows.

Research Interests

  • Retinal neurodegeneration & optic nerve regeneration
  • Extracellular vesicles in therapy
  • Gene therapy & cell therapy
  • Multi-omics & bioinformatics
  • Hematologic malignancies
  • Molecular biology & imaging
